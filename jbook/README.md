This website supplements the course materials for the 2026-2027 "Optimisation and Data Sciences" module on the [Master Ingénierie des Systèmes Complexes](fst.univ-lorraine.fr/formations/master-ingenierie-des-systemes-complexes) at the university of Lorraine.

> An overview of the key mathematical concepts behind optimisation and data sciences

:::{warning}Work in progress
These lecture notes are currently being compiled and may be updated. Any feedback or comments would be welcome.
:::

:::{hint}Objectives
*Optimisation*:
- to be familiar with the classification of optimisation problems
- to be able to provide theoretical criteria for the optimality of a problem
- to have a good grasp of first- and second-order optimisation algorithms

*Data Sciences*:
- to master matrix decomposition algorithms (ICA,NMF,SVD)
- to understand and be able to implement support vector machines
- to be familiar with the basics of neural networks (architectures, backpropagation, ...)
:::

## Course Organisation
- Speakers: E.H. Djermoune, P. Catala
- 20h Optimisation, divided into CM (6h), TD (6h) and TP (6h-8h)
- 20h Data Sciences, divided into CM (6h), TD(6h) et TP (8h-10h)
- The course materials are available on [Arche](https://arche.univ-lorraine.fr/) (slides, exercises sheets, practical sessions)

:::{note}Pre-requisites
- Basic linear algebra: vector spaces, matrix calculus
- Basic analysis: continuity, differentiability
:::

## Evaluation
- Written exam (2 hours)
